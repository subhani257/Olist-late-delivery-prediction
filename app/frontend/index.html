About the System

Trained and validated on real-world Brazilian e-commerce operations data.

📦 Dataset & Domain

Built on the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist containing 99k+ anonymised orders from 2016 to 2018. The dataset integrates 9 relational CSV files detailing customer & seller geolocation, product physical characteristics, order timestamps, and payment structures.

🛡️ Strict Leakage Prevention

To guarantee real-world usability at order placement, all post-dispatch features (carrier pickup dates, actual delivery timestamps, and customer review scores) are strictly excluded from prediction inputs.

About the Olist Risk Predictor

End-to-end data mining architecture, feature engineering, and model evaluation pipeline.

🎯

Problem Statement

Olist connects small Brazilian merchants to major e-commerce platforms. A significant percentage of shipments suffer from carrier delays. This early-warning classifier estimates late delivery probability at order placement so support & logistics teams can take proactive measures.

📊

Dataset & Preprocessing

Utilizes ~99,000 real anonymized orders (2016–2018) from Kaggle. Cleaned relational schemas cover customer & seller ZIP geolocations, Haversine distance calculations, package volumetric metrics, and payment installment features.

🚫

Strict Leakage Prevention

Features recorded post-checkout (such as carrier dispatch timestamp, delivery receipt timestamp, or customer review scores) are excluded to prevent target leakage and ensure true operational utility before dispatch.

⚙️

ML Model Pipeline

Evaluated Logistic Regression, Random Forest, LightGBM, and MLP Neural Networks. The champion MLP Neural Net delivers optimal ranking ability with a 0.7766 ROC-AUC score across 44 standardized input variables.
