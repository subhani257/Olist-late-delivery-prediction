Slide 06

Turning Raw Noise into Clean Predictive Signals

1. CLEAN & GEOLOCATE
  • Resolved 19,015 ZIP prefixes to median centroids.
  • Calculated straight-line **Haversine Distance (km)** from seller to customer.
  • Normalized city text and mapped 27 states to 5 macro-regions.
2. FEATURE ENGINEERING
  • `freight_to_price_ratio`: Relative logistics cost intensity.
  • `estimated_delivery_days`: Days promised at checkout.
  • `approval_lag_hours`: Seller order confirmation turnaround time.
  • `distance_x_items`: Cross-interaction signal.
3. TIME-SERIES SPLIT
  • No random shuffling (prevents data leakage).
  • **Train (70%):** 67,529 earliest orders.
  • **Val (15%):** 14,470 orders for tuning.
  • **Test (15%):** 14,471 unseen final validation orders.
